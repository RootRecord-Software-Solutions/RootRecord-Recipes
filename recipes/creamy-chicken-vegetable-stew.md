# Creamy Chicken & Vegetable Stew

A hearty, creamy chicken-and-vegetable stew with potatoes, carrots, green beans, onion, and tender canned chicken. The trick is **saving the vegetable cooking water** and using it to bring together cream of mushroom soup and turkey gravy, so none of that flavor goes to waste.

This is a *cook-by-feel* recipe: the vegetables and chicken weren't measured during the original meal. The amounts below are only specified where Alex gave them.

## Ingredients

- Green beans, amount to taste
- Potatoes, cut into chunks
- Carrots, cut into pieces
- Onion, chopped or cut as preferred
- Canned chicken, amount to taste
- **1 quart chicken broth**
- **1 can cream of mushroom soup**
- **1 packet turkey gravy mix**
- Water, enough to boil the vegetables

## How Alex Made It

1. Put the green beans, cut potatoes, carrots, and onion in a pot with enough water to boil them. Cook until the vegetables reach the tenderness you like.
2. **Save some of the vegetable cooking water**, then drain the vegetables. Do not discard all the flavored water.
3. Add the canned chicken and **1 quart of chicken broth** to the cooked vegetables.
4. In a separate bowl or container, combine the reserved vegetable cooking water with **1 can of cream of mushroom soup** and **1 packet of turkey gravy mix**. Stir thoroughly to work out the lumps.
5. Stir that creamy mushroom-and-turkey-gravy mixture into the chicken and vegetables. Heat gently, stirring, until everything is hot and the broth becomes creamy and stew-like. Adjust the amount of reserved vegetable water used to get the thickness you prefer.
6. Serve hot, with plenty of vegetables, chicken, and creamy broth in each bowl.

## Flavor and Texture

Comforting, savory, and substantial. Potato chunks make it filling; carrots and green beans add color and a little sweetness; onion rounds out the vegetable broth. The cream of mushroom and turkey gravy packet give the liquid a rich, almost chicken-pot-pie-like texture.

## Serve With

**Dinner rolls or cornbread** are Alex's usual preferred accompaniments to a stew like this, especially for dipping into the creamy broth. The original photographed bowl was served **without either**, because he was out of both.

## Notes From the First Bowl

- Alex boiled the vegetables in water first and used the **saved vegetable cooking water** as part of the mushroom-soup/turkey-gravy mixture.
- The **1 quart chicken broth**, **1 can cream of mushroom**, and **1 packet turkey gravy** are explicitly stated amounts. All other quantities, simmer duration, and seasoning choices were not measured or specified.
- This is a practical pantry-based meal using canned chicken and convenient soup/gravy ingredients, not a from-scratch stock recipe.
