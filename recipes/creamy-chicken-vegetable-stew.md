# Creamy Chicken & Vegetable Stew

Tender potatoes, carrots, green beans, onion, and chicken in a creamy mushroom-and-gravy broth. The key technique is to **mix the soup and gravy separately**, then fold the mixture into the vegetables so they keep their shape.

## Ingredients

- Green beans, amount to taste
- Potatoes, cut into chunks
- Carrots, cut into pieces
- Onion, chopped
- Canned chicken, amount to taste
- 1 quart chicken broth
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 packet turkey gravy mix
- Water, enough to cook the vegetables

**Flexible quantities:** Except for the broth, soup, and gravy mix, the amounts are intentionally left to preference.

## Instructions

1. Put the green beans, potatoes, carrots, and onion in a pot with enough water to cover them. Cook until the vegetables reach your preferred tenderness.
2. Reserve some of the vegetable cooking water, then drain the vegetables.
3. Add the canned chicken and chicken broth to the cooked vegetables.
4. **In a separate bowl**, whisk the reserved vegetable water with the cream of mushroom soup and turkey gravy mix until smooth. Mixing separately avoids breaking up the tender vegetables.
5. Pour the creamy mixture into the pot. Fold or stir gently, just enough to combine.
6. Heat until the stew is hot throughout. Add a little more reserved vegetable water if you prefer a thinner broth.
7. Serve warm.

## Serving ideas

Serve with dinner rolls or cornbread for dipping. The potatoes make the stew filling, while the carrots, green beans, and onion add texture and flavor.

## Cooking tips

- Save the vegetable cooking water; it helps carry flavor into the final broth.
- Stir gently once the potatoes are soft.
- Use the reserved cooking water to adjust the thickness without adding another ingredient.
- This is a practical pantry-based stew that works well with canned chicken.
