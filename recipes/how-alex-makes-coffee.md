## Version 1 — Official hot coffee, concentrate first

Alex's earlier, photo-demonstrated routine was preserved in Ava-Ivy as **“Alex's Official Coffee Protocol.”** This method is **not** the recycled Pepsi-bottle version.

### Ingredients

- **Dehydrated coffee** — Alex's name for instant coffee; quantity unspecified
- **Sugar** — official measurement: **“a fuck ton of sugar”** (his words)
- **Evaporated milk** — poured from a can, to taste; Great Value was shown in the original demonstration
- **Hot water** — heated separately, quantity unspecified
- **Vanilla** — when available
- **Pumpkin spice** — a favored optional flavor

### How Alex makes it

1. Heat water separately.
2. Use a reusable cup (the photo-demonstrated example was a repurposed McDonald's cup).
3. Add the dehydrated/instant coffee and a fuck ton of sugar.
4. Add evaporated milk.
5. **Stir the coffee, sugar, and evaporated milk together first** to create a creamy concentrate.
6. **Add the hot water last.**
7. Enjoy; use vanilla and/or pumpkin spice when available.

**Do not swap the order and claim it's the same recipe.** The concentrate-first step was demonstrated and corrected in the original record. No numerical ingredient measurements were given.

## Version 2 — Recycled Pepsi-Bottle Coffee (No-Heat Edition)

**Alex's October 20 description, verbatim:**

> "The coffee maker is a recycled pepsi bottle, sugar, instant coffee, and room temp water"

This version is about making coffee without needing to heat water. No milk, vanilla, or spice was mentioned for *this* preparation.

### Ingredients and equipment — confirmed

- A **recycled Pepsi bottle** used as the mixing container
- **Sugar**, amount not specified for the bottle version
- **Instant coffee**, amount not specified
- **Room-temperature water**, amount not specified

### Practical outline

1. Use a **clean, food-safe bottle** and drinking-safe, room-temperature water.
2. Combine the instant coffee, sugar, and water in the bottle.
3. Mix until the coffee and sugar are dissolved; shaking with the cap securely closed is an **unverified practical inference**, not a documented step Alex explicitly described.
4. Drink or pour out of the bottle as preferred; how Alex serves it was **not specified**.

**Not confirmed:** Bottle size; ingredient quantities; exact mixing order; whether he shakes or stirs; how he drinks/serves it; whether he adds milk or flavorings on other occasions. Do not invent measurements or merge the hot-coffee order into this version.

**Food-safety distinction:** Room-temperature water can be used for instant coffee, but it still needs to be **safe for drinking**. Untreated rainwater isn't automatically safe to drink. If collected rainwater is the source, it needs appropriate treatment and safe storage before it goes into coffee; boiling alone does not remove chemical contamination. Allow water to cool before putting it in a plastic soda bottle. These are safe-handling notes, **not claims that Alex used or skipped any particular treatment**.
